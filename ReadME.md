## Hackathon challenges

### Challenge 1: Unlocking Career Progression for Women in Tech
#### Problem Statement:
Despite the tech sector’s rapid growth, women remain significantly underrepresented - comprising just 28.2% of the global tech workforce, many face unclear career pathways, limited access to mentorship, and confidence barriers such as imposter syndrome. These challenges are exacerbated by a lack of visibility into role progression and skill development opportunities. Participants are invited to design inclusive digital solutions that empower women to navigate their careers with clarity and confidence - through personalised guidance, real-time feedback, and supportive networks.
#### Key Areas to Consider:
Mapping career trajectories tailored to women’s experiences and aspirations.
Identifying skill gaps and connecting to relevant learning resources.
Building confidence through peer support, micro-coaching, and goal tracking.
Facilitating mentorship and sponsorship opportunities for women in tech.
#### Expected Outcomes:
Tools that visualise personalised, inclusive career pathways.
Platforms that encourage skill development and confidence-building.
Solutions that foster mentorship, community, and visibility for women in tech.
 
 

### Challenge 2: Advancing Gender and Age Equity in Tech
#### Problem Statement:
Gender and age biases continue to shape hiring and promotion decisions in tech. According to McKinsey’s 2024 report, for every 100 men promoted to manager, only 89 women are promoted. This gap is even wider for women of colour and older women, contributing to underrepresentation in leadership and amplifying imposter syndrome. Participants are challenged to create tools that detect and reduce bias, promote transparency, and build inclusive support systems that uplift women across all career stages.
#### Key Areas to Consider:
Identifying and mitigating unconscious bias in recruitment and promotion.
Visualising representation data and tracking progress toward equity goals.
Creating safe, empowering spaces for women to share experiences and access mentorship.
Supporting mid- and late-career women navigating systemic barriers.
#### Expected Outcomes:
Bias-detection tools for hiring and performance processes.
Dashboards that track and visualise gender and age diversity metrics.
Peer-support platforms that foster resilience, confidence, and community.
